How Iron Insights calculates strength percentiles

Iron Insights is built on one idea: compare a lifter only against people like them. Every percentile, strength standard and sex comparison is derived directly from 2.9 million competition results in the OpenPowerlifting database — no hand-tuned charts or rules of thumb. This page documents the method.

Matched cohorts

A lift is never ranked against everyone at once. Iron Insights groups results by sex, weight class, equipment (raw, single-ply, multi-ply, wraps and so on), tested status and age band, then ranks your number inside that cohort. A 75 kg raw tested lifter is measured against other 75 kg raw tested lifters.

Percentiles and standards

Within a cohort, each lift sits in a histogram of competition results. Your percentile is the share of that cohort you exceed. The published strength standards are read off the same distribution: the median (p50) is the middle lifter and the 90th percentile (p90) marks a strong one. Because they come from real results, the standards update automatically as the dataset grows.

Bodyweight-adjusted scoring

To compare lifters of different sizes, totals are also scored with DOTS, Wilks and Goodlift (GL) coefficients. These formulas weight a total against bodyweight so a 60 kg and a 120 kg lifter land on one scale.

Data and refresh

The source is the public-domain OpenPowerlifting results database, currently 2,935,842 individual squat, bench and deadlift results (data current as of 31 July 2026). The dataset is refreshed on a roughly weekly cadence and every figure on the site is recomputed from it.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

All figures are computed from OpenPowerlifting, the public-domain competition results database. Iron Insights is independent and not affiliated with OpenPowerlifting.

How often is it updated?

The published dataset is refreshed on a roughly weekly cadence, and percentiles, standards and comparisons are recomputed each time.

Does my data leave my device?

No. The calculators run entirely in your browser; your numbers are not uploaded or stored.
