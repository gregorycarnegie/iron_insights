Barbell plate calculator: what plates do I load?

A plate calculator tells you which discs to put on each side of the bar to hit a target weight. Enter the target and Iron Insights subtracts the bar and collars, then picks the fewest plates per side — in kilograms or pounds.

Try the live tool →

How loading is worked out

The maths is simple: (target − bar − collars) ÷ 2 is the load per side, which is then filled with the largest plates first. A standard men's bar is 20 kg, a women's bar is 15 kg, and competition collars add 2.5 kg each.

Worked example

For a 180 kg target on a 20 kg bar with no collars, you need 80 kg per side: 2×20 kg + 2×15 kg + 1×10 kg. The visual loader shows the colour-coded plates and flags if a target cannot be made exactly.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a barbell weigh?

A standard Olympic men's bar is 20 kg (44 lb); a women's bar is 15 kg (33 lb). The calculator lets you pick the bar and whether you are using collars.

What plates do I need for 100kg?

On a 20 kg bar that is 40 kg per side: 2×20 kg, or 1×20 + 1×15 + 1×5. The tool shows the optimal set for any target.
