What percentile is my squat, bench, deadlift or total?

Iron Insights ranks your lift against more than 2.9 million competition results from the OpenPowerlifting database. Enter your bodyweight and lifts and it returns your exact percentile within your sex, weight-class and equipment cohort, then maps it to a strength tier from Novice to Legend.

How the percentile is calculated

Your number is compared against the distribution of a matched cohort — same sex, weight class, equipment (raw, single-ply, etc.), tested status and age band — not against everyone at once. For the total you can rank by raw kilograms or by the DOTS, Wilks or Goodlift (GL) bodyweight-adjusted scores.

Worked examples from the live data:

  • A 83 kg male lifter (raw, all ages) with a 180 kg squat sits at roughly the 44th percentile of that cohort.
  • A 83 kg male lifter (raw, all ages) with a 230 kg squat sits at roughly the 90th percentile of that cohort.
  • A 63 kg female lifter (raw, all ages) with a 150 kg deadlift sits at roughly the 80th percentile of that cohort.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good powerlifting total?

It depends on bodyweight, sex and equipment. Iron Insights treats anything above the 80th percentile of your cohort as Advanced and above the 95th as Elite. Compare against your own weight class rather than an absolute number.

How are the percentiles worked out?

Each lift is placed in the distribution of lifters who share your sex, weight class, equipment, tested status and age band, using competition results from OpenPowerlifting.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no signup and nothing to install — it runs entirely in your browser.
