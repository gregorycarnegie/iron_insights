Body fat percentage calculator

Iron Insights estimates body fat percentage four ways — the US Navy tape method, the YMCA waist method, and the Jackson-Pollock 3- and 7-site skinfold equations — and reports the resulting fat mass and lean mass. Each method trades convenience for accuracy.

The methods

  • US Navy — tape measurements of neck, waist (and hips for women) plus height. Quick and equipment-free.
  • YMCA — waist circumference and bodyweight. The simplest estimate.
  • Jackson-Pollock 3-site — skinfold calipers at three sites plus age. More accurate with good technique.
  • Jackson-Pollock 7-site — seven skinfold sites for the tightest estimate.

A note on accuracy

Every field method is an estimate with several percentage points of error. Use one method consistently and track the trend rather than treating any single reading as exact. DEXA and hydrostatic weighing remain the laboratory references.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most accurate way to measure body fat?

In a field setting, multi-site skinfolds (Jackson-Pollock) with good caliper technique tend to beat tape methods. DEXA scans are the practical gold standard but require a clinic.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Broadly, fitness ranges sit around 14-20% for men and 21-27% for women, with athletes often lower. Ranges shift with age and the estimation method.
